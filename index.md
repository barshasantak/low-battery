## 📖 The Product Story

### *Why we built Low-Battery*

Managing screen time for young children is one of the most exhausting battlegrounds of modern parenting:

* **Tantrums and negotiation loops:** Setting a verbal limit often leads to tears, bargaining for "just five more minutes," and emotional friction.
* **Parent as the adversary:** Taking the phone away places parents in the role of the "fun-stopper," creating conflict rather than peaceful closure.
* **Complex timer apps:** Built-in Screen Time limits and parental control apps flash jarring system popups, require passcode overrides, or abruptly cut off mid-activity.
* **Universal hardware understanding:** Children may not respect parental boundaries on device time, but they intuitively understand and accept physical hardware limits: **when the battery is empty, the device must rest.**

We asked a simple question: **What if parents had an instant, authentic, and peaceful way to show that the iPhone is out of power—turning a potential conflict into a natural transition?**

Low-Battery was created at **Tara Design Studio** to answer that need. Built entirely in native Swift and SwiftUI, Low-Battery presents an authentic, full-screen critical charge alert (<2%) that feels identical to Apple’s system shutdown screen. No arguments. No tears. Just a gentle conclusion to screen time.

## ✨ User Experience Highlights

### 1. The 3-Second Transition
When screen time is over, open Low-Battery and show the screen to your child. They see the battery warning, recognize that the device cannot play any more videos or games, and willingly hand the device back to be "charged."

### 2. Native System Immersion
Built using pure vector geometry and native typography, the battery graphic looks and feels like an integral part of iOS. Status indicators and home bars automatically fade away.

### 3. Smart Size-Class Adaptation
Whether your child is playing a game in landscape or watching a clip in portrait, Low-Battery detects the orientation and renders a clean, unclipped warning that respects all display boundaries.

### 4. Zero Data Footprint
No analytics, no subscriptions, no tracking, and no external network calls. It performs its single task instantly and quietly.
